It comes down to two numbers.

Supply
Usage
Supply rate

A fixed cost you pay every day just to be connected, even if you use nothing. Like line rental.

Typically around $1.10 a day in NSW
Usage rate

What you pay for each unit of energy you actually use. The meter ticking.

Typically around 39c per kWh in NSW
For a typical Sydney home, the regulator's benchmark is about $1,965 a year on 3,900 kWh. Your two numbers are what put you above or below that.

There's a catch too ↓

Then there's the welcome offer.

Up to $200 off to sign you up. But that plan often carries a higher ongoing rate. They give you the discount to hook you in, because they know they'll make it back, and more, over the years you stay.

The flashy plan

$200 welcome credit, but 42c per kWh.

The plainer plan

No credit, but 30c per kWh.

On a typical home the plainer plan can win by hundreds over a year, even without the credit. That's the confusion they count on.
