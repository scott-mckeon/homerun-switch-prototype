Show me ↓

Two numbers: supply rate and usage rate.

Supply
Usage
Supply rate

A fixed cost you pay every day just to be connected, even if you use nothing. Like line rental.

Typically around $1.10 a day in NSW
Usage rate

What you pay for each unit of energy you actually use. The meter ticking.

Typically around 39c per kWh in NSW
For a typical Sydney home, the regulator's benchmark is about $1,965 a year on 3,900 kWh. Your two numbers are what put you above or below that.
