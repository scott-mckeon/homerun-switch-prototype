Object 3 · The one next step

See ENGIE's full plan details, then confirm

You see every rate, fee and the cooling off terms before anything happens. Confirming on the next screen is where you give consent. You then have 10 business days to change your mind.

Rule in playConsent needs full disclosure before it counts. The tap opens the plan details and confirm step where CIMET captures consent. It is not a one tap switch straight off the card, and there is no standing authority to switch them again later.

Touchpoint 2

Immediately after the first switch

Turn a one off switch into an ongoing relationship. Kill the post switch anxiety, set the six monthly rhythm, and protect them from switching again too early and forfeiting the credit. This is where the account is born.

Confirmation

You are switching to ENGIE

Nice work. Here is exactly what happens now, so there are no surprises.

  • 1

    ENGIE handles the switch

    Your new retailer completes it through the market systems. You do not need to call anyone or contact EnergyAustralia.

  • 2

    One final bill from EnergyAustralia

    Then your bills come from ENGIE. No overlap, no double charge.

  • 3

    10 business days to change your mind

    Cooling off. No penalty, no exit fee if you pull out.

  • 4

    Do nothing until month 6

    We will tell you when it is worth looking again.
